# Norway offshore wind O&M — strategy reference

Read by `find-jobs` and `tailor-application` on this branch to focus the search and framing on Norway specifically, instead of the general `europe` track sweep.

## The constraint
Norway is EEA/Schengen but **not EU** — that doesn't help a non-EU applicant. The route in is the **Skilled Worker residence permit** (UDI, Oppholdstillatelse som faglært): a qualifying job offer is required *before* applying, employer submits a digital job-offer confirmation, salary floor ~NOK 599,200/yr for master's-level roles (~NOK 522,600 bachelor's), processing 4–8 weeks. No confirmed general job-seeker visa route for this profile — verify directly with UDI before relying on one. Source: [udi.no — Skilled workers](https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/work-immigration/skilled-workers/).

**Implication for search strategy**: direct outreach and a targeted pitch matter more than volume applications, because there's effectively one path to an offer and it has to come first.
