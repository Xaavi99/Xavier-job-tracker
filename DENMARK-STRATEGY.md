# Denmark offshore wind O&M — strategy reference

Read by `find-jobs` and `tailor-application` on this branch to focus the search and framing on Denmark specifically, instead of the general `europe` track sweep.

## The constraint
Denmark has **no general skilled-worker visa** — both routes are employer-sponsored via SIRI (Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration), meaning a qualifying job offer has to come first:
- **Pay Limit Scheme (Beløbsordningen)**: DKK 552,000/yr (regular — any role qualifies, no title check) or DKK 446,000/yr (supplementary — role must be publicly advertised, stricter conditions).
- **Positive List for Higher Education**: a list of qualifying job titles, refreshed twice yearly (Jan/July) — a "wind power" / energy engineer listing is reported by secondary sources but **not confirmed on the live official list**, verify at [nyidanmark.dk](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/pl-PL/You-want-to-apply/Work/Pay-limit-scheme) before relying on it.

IDA's (Danish engineers' association) 2026 indicative MSc-graduate minimum is DKK 47,400/month → **DKK 568,800/yr**, just above the regular Pay Limit Scheme threshold — an on-scale MSc-level offer should qualify under the regular scheme without needing the Positive List at all. [IDA — indicative minimum salary](https://ida.dk/en/career-and-legal-advice/salary/indicative-minimum-salary-for-stem-professionals-in-denmark)

**Implication for search strategy**: unlike Norway's single-employer market, Denmark has real breadth — this is a volume-and-fit play across several employers, using the same general RBI/reliability framing that already works for the UK CVs, not a single tailored pitch.

## Target employers
**Ørsted** (world's largest offshore wind developer) and **Vestas** (turbine OEM, strong service/O&M arm) are the anchors. Also active: **Siemens Gamesa** (Brande site), **Vattenfall**, **RWE Offshore Wind**, **Global Wind Service** (O&M service contractor), **Ramboll** (asset-integrity/reliability engineering consultancy). 80-95+ offshore-wind/engineering roles are live on Glassdoor at any given time — ongoing hiring, not a one-off.

## Certifications
GWO Basic Safety Training package (~£920-1,110 inc. VAT, obtainable in the UK now). Industry-standard everywhere, not Denmark-specific.

## Language reality
[JobsInEnglish.dk](https://jobsinenglish.dk/category/engineering/) lists ~427 live English-language engineering roles in Denmark. Ørsted/Vestas hire internationally at engineering level; Danish is more likely to matter for site-technician roles than office-based reliability/O&M-planning work — directional, not company-confirmed, so don't rule out a role solely for not stating "English" explicitly.

## Job boards
[JobsInEnglish.dk](https://jobsinenglish.dk/category/engineering/), [Jobindex.dk](https://www.jobboardfinder.com/jobboard-jobindexdk-denmark) (weak English UI but Denmark's largest board), company career pages direct (Ørsted, Vestas, Siemens Gamesa, Vattenfall, RWE, Global Wind Service, Ramboll), IDA for salary benchmarking.

## Salary reference
Wind Energy Engineer average ≈ DKK 622,000/yr (range 428k-760k), roughly £70-72k. IDA all-engineer median ≈ DKK 624,000/yr — comfortably above the Pay Limit Scheme threshold.
